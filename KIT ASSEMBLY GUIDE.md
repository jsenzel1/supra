Aseembly Guide is coming!!! sorry for the delay 

I'll include some pictures of an assembled module for the time being - if you've built a few kits it should be pretty straightforward 

The one big exception to this is the joystick! it's easy, but needs to be soldered a little weird. Instead of pushing it all the way throught he holes you want to line it up with the circuit board logo side down on a work surface/table, and stick it in, so that it hits the work surface (NOTE: ORIENTATION MATTERS, there is a dot on the circuit board, and a dot on one of the corners of the joystick. the joystick should be placed so that the dots line up with each other). then tack down one of the legs by soldering it on this side. Then flip the board over and solder all 6 legs correctly. 
![IMG_3765 Large](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/df24f981-ad2e-4017-888f-9ba60f16b020)
![IMG_3759 Large](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/9b80c5be-75d1-41d0-8ebb-c3a1709453a7)

for the screen, place the legs into the holes in the board, and then place the standoff (there's a hole in the pcb for it to be screwed in) then put the screw in (it will be in the top left corner, its not there in this pic) 
then while the screen is held correctly in place solder the holes on the back (make sure it looks correct, flat. before soldering. The legs shouldn't protrude out the back of the board)
![IMG_3762 Large](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/8a655d6c-7a6b-4754-90b5-2c4780e1b76a)

the back has the standoff nuts, the shrouded header (note orientation if kit comes with shrouded) and the firmware flash  button 
![IMG_3761 Large](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/e311c42c-daf4-46c0-bf29-06a2b66e8edc)

the jacks and buttons are pretty straight forward normal stuff

notes: theres' another standoff which you will screw the panel into at the end

lightpipes are just pushed in through the front. can be done at the end 
![IMG_3766 Large](https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/574a9c3c-f325-496c-93d3-ee4d95c823ae)

little black rubber thing is the cap for the joystick - just stick it on 

battery goes in + side up

if firmware not flashed: hold down the backside button, while plugging in a usb c cable (other side connected to a computer) the module will show up like a thumb drive on your computer - drag in the supra.uf2 file from the firmware directory of this repo. May take a little bit to upload, like 30 seconds to a minute.
